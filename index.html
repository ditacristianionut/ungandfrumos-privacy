Un Gând Frumos — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

Contact: dci.development.121@gmail.com.

How the app works

Un Gând Frumos provides images with Romanian greeting messages for sharing. The collection works offline. We do not require an account or request access to your contacts, conversations, location, or personal photos. The app is intended for adults.

Data stored on your device

The app stores your favorites, selected themes, viewed-card history, preferred greeting font, optional usage measurement setting, morning reminder setting and time, advertising choice and its timestamp, and confirmed Premium purchase status on your device. These settings personalize your collection and preserve your choices between sessions.

To apply the limit of five free shares per day, the app stores a date and usage counter locally. The limit follows your device’s local date; Premium allows unlimited sharing. The counter increases when the app opens a destination app or the Android sharing menu, rather than when you simply open the app’s own sharing selector. If opening the destination fails and the app detects the error, the usage is refunded. Canceling later in the destination app or Android sharing menu does not confirm delivery and does not automatically refund usage. The counter contains no recipients or conversations and is not sent to a server operated by us.

Preview and sharing images are generated on your device and stored in the app’s private cache. You can clear the cache through Android settings. App backup and data transfer are disabled through the Android configuration. Clearing the app’s data removes local data but does not delete data already processed by service providers. Purchases can be restored using the same Google Play account.

Sharing and the clipboard

When you tap a share button, the app displays a selector of compatible installed apps. To build this selector, it checks locally for WhatsApp, WhatsApp Business, Messenger, Facebook, Instagram, Telegram, Viber, and Signal, and reads their names and icons from Android. This sharing feature does not inventory all installed apps or send the checked app list to a server operated by us or to Analytics. Separate processing by the advertising SDK is described below.

You can select an app directly or choose “More” to open Android’s sharing menu. The selected image is made available to the destination app through a temporary read permission; this does not grant general access to the app’s private files. Destination apps and social platforms apply their own privacy policies. We do not know the recipients, conversations, or whether sharing was completed. Copying a message places the selected text in the Android clipboard so you can paste it into another app.

Advertising

Start.io provides native advertisements within the collection. The app asks for an explicit advertising choice before initializing the advertising SDK and stores your choice and its timestamp locally. If you accept, Start.io and its partners may collect, use, and share the advertising identifier, IP address, information about your device, connection, and installed apps, and advertising interactions for personalization, measurement, and fraud prevention. Approximate location may be inferred from the IP address or connection. The app does not request precise location or Bluetooth permission. Data may be processed outside the European Economic Area.

You can decline or change your choice in Settings under Advertising and privacy. Declining stops advertising requests from this app and sends a non-personalization signal to the SDK if it has already started. On subsequent launches after declining, the SDK is not initialized. Reading, favorites, and sharing remain available. Start.io may display its own additional notice or consent form. Declining in this app does not automatically delete previously collected data or change choices given to the provider through other apps or services.

For details about data, partners, transfers, and rights, see the Start.io Privacy Policy. The provider also offers data and sharing choices.

Optional usage measurement

Google Analytics for Firebase is disabled by default. When configured and enabled by you, it processes usage events, installation identifiers, technical information, and session statistics. Events include viewed images, favorites, previews, opening the sharing selector, and purchase confirmation. Greeting-related events may include the greeting’s catalog identifier. Opening the sharing selector does not prove that an image was sent or delivered. These events do not include conversation content, recipients, search terms, or the selected destination app. Analytics advertising signals are disabled. Turning measurement off stops future collection without automatically deleting data already collected. More information is available in the Firebase privacy documentation.

Purchases and reminders

Premium is a one-time purchase handled through Google Play. Google Play handles payments and financial information; the app does not receive your payment card details. The app verifies Google Play’s signed response locally, acknowledges the purchase, and stores your entitlement status. We do not operate a purchase server.

Morning reminders are optional and scheduled locally at your chosen time, with notification permission where Android requires it. The chosen time is stored on your device. You can turn reminders off in the app’s Settings or through Android. We do not operate a remote notification service.
